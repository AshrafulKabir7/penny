How to ask

Press Alt+Shift+P or click Penny's toolbar button, then type your question, or press the mic and talk. Say "this" while pointing with the mouse, or circle something as you talk. Esc clears the drawings.

Privacy

Penny only looks at a page when you ask it something there. Then it sends a screenshot of the visible part of that tab, its title and your question to the provider you chose, and nothing anywhere else. Penny has no server and no analytics.
